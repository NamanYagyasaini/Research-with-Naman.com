Research Interests

Where entanglement meets the early universe

Primary interests in quantum information and computing, with cosmology and particle phenomenology as the physical settings I apply them to.

01 — Primary

Quantum Information & Computing

Entanglement structure of quantum states, quantum simulation of many-body systems, and quantum error correction. My thesis applies entanglement measures (entropy, negativity) to squeezed cosmological states, treating the early universe as a natural laboratory for quantum information questions.

Entanglement Entropy Negativity Squeezed States Many-Body Simulation Error Correction
02 — Application Area

Cosmology & Inflation

The Mukhanov–Sasaki formalism for cosmological perturbations, the quantum-to-classical transition of primordial fluctuations, and Starobinsky inflation as a concrete model in which these questions become calculable.

Mukhanov–Sasaki Starobinsky Inflation Primordial Perturbations
03 — Secondary

Particle Cosmology & Dark Matter

Thermal freeze-out dynamics and relic density calculations for dark matter candidates, connecting Boltzmann-equation solutions to observational constraints — developed during a research internship at IIT Guwahati.

Thermal Freeze-Out Relic Density Boltzmann Equations
04 — Future Directions

Open Quantum Systems & HEP

Longer-term interest in open quantum systems and experimental high-energy physics as adjacent areas — reflected in applications to schools such as the ICTP Open Quantum Systems programme and winter schools in experimental HEP.

Open Quantum Systems Experimental HEP Nuclear Structure
