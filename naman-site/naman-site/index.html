Research Statement

A growing academic portfolio

My work sits at the intersection of cosmology and quantum information — using entanglement as a lens to understand how the classical universe emerges from quantum initial conditions. Alongside this, I bring a phenomenology background from dark matter freeze-out calculations, and a long-term interest in quantum simulation of many-body systems and error correction.

Primary

Cosmology & QI

Entanglement structure of cosmological perturbations; squeezed states; quantum-to-classical transition.

Secondary

Particle Cosmology

Dark matter thermal freeze-out, relic density, and Boltzmann-equation phenomenology.

Long-term

Quantum Simulation

Quantum simulation of many-body systems and quantum error correction as future research directions.
